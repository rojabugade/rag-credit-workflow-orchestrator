# Synthetic Commercial Lending Policy v1
Effective: 2026-01-01. Illustrative internal policy only; not law or regulatory guidance.

## 4.2 Documentation
Complete financial statements and collateral evidence are required before a final human decision.

## 5.1 Illustrative risk thresholds
DSCR below 1.20, debt-to-equity above 2.00, current ratio below 1.10, or LTV above 0.80 require additional human scrutiny. These thresholds are configurable and not universal standards.
